# Newton's laws

Forces describe interactions. Their combined effect determines how motion
changes.

<Grid>
  <Definition title="Second law">
    The net force equals mass multiplied by acceleration.
  </Definition>

  <Formula wide>
    $$\sum \vec{F}=m\vec{a}$$
  </Formula>

  <Warning>
    Add forces as vectors. Their directions matter.
  </Warning>

  <Example>
    For a mass of $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ under a net force of $6\,\mathrm{N}$,
    the acceleration is $3\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$.
  </Example>
</Grid>
